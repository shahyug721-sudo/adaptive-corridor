How this model works

Vehicles follow the Intelligent Driver Model (IDM). On dispatch, an alert propagates — ~75 m by siren/line-of-sight, or ~420 m instantly via V2V radio. Alerted drivers in lane 1 pull left, lanes 2–3 pull right, opening a corridor between lanes 1 and 2 (the European Rettungsgasse rule). In V2X mode the signal controller also preempts to green as the unit approaches. The compliance slider injects drivers who don't move until the siren is directly behind them — watch them glow red and block the corridor.
